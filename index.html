Core Platform

in one place.

Policy Management

Centralise every client policy with full detail tracking — insurer, premium, coverage type, payment status, and more. Find anything in seconds.

Renewal Tracking

Commissions Engine

Set custom commission rates per policy type, track earned vs. pending commissions, and export monthly statements with one click.
